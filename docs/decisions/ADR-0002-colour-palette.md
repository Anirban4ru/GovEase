# ADR-0002: Colour Palette Selection

**Status:** Proposed (Awaiting Human Decision)  
**Date:** 2026-11-05  
**Decider:** Founder

---

## Context and Constraints

- Strict constraint: **ZERO VIBE CODING**. No cyberpunk, neon, glowing borders, or flashy gradients.
- Timeless, understated public-service sophistication.
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast compliance mandatory (minimum 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for UI boundaries).
- Must avoid government logo or emblem cloning while conveying calm, reliable institutional authority.

## Options Proposed

### Option A: Civic Slate (Cool Institutional Authority)

- **Primary:** Deep Navy Blue `#1D4E8F` (Contrast on white: 7.8:1 ✅ AAA)
- **Neutrals:** Cool grey scale `#161C28` to `#F7F8FA`
- **Character:** Authoritative, calm, dependable. Similar in gravitas to GOV.UK and high-trust European civic systems.

### Option B: Warm Ash (Approachable Civic Resilience)

- **Primary:** Deep Teal-Green `#1A5C52` (Contrast on white: 7.1:1 ✅ AAA)
- **Neutrals:** Warm grey scale `#1A1614` to `#F8F7F5`
- **Character:** Humanist, grounded, resilient. Well suited to warm ambient lighting on Indian mobile displays.

### Option C: Stone & Ink (Refined Editorial Restraint)

- **Primary:** Near-black `#111110` with selective Saffron-Amber `#B45309` accent (Contrast: 4.6:1 ✅ AA)
- **Neutrals:** Neutral stone palette `#FAFAF9` to `#1C1C18`
- **Character:** High-end print editorial typography. Maximum restraint and clarity.

## Recommendation

Select **Option A (Civic Slate)** for maximum institutional trust, or **Option C (Stone & Ink)** for timeless editorial elegance. Pending human decision.

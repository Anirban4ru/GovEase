## 1. Principles of Originality

Per Section 0.3 and Section 1.5 of `Design System Specification`:

1. **Zero Copying of Official Symbols:** No use of the State Emblem of India (Lion Capital), no tricolour flag banners used as logos, and no reproduction of any official ministry or department trademarks.
2. **Field Map Abstraction:** All visual textures are inspired by the precision of cartography, land surveying, contour maps, and traditional geometric folk line-art (Warli geometric lines and Kolam grid geometry), interpreted through pure modern minimalism.
3. **Restrained Presence:** Motifs are used exclusively as low-opacity background textures ($\le 4\%$ opacity), never behind body text, ensuring zero interference with reading comprehension or contrast ratios.

## 2. Motif Set Inventory

### 2.1. The Six-Node Radial Mark (Platform Identity Motif)

- **Geometry:** A central circular node representing the citizen ("You are here"), surrounded by six symmetrically positioned nodes connected with fine radial grid lines ($0.75\text{px}$ stroke).
- **Symbolism:** Represents the six fundamental spheres of everyday life (Healthcare, Education, Finance, Citizen Identity, Mobility, Utilities) surrounding and serving the citizen.
- **Application:** Used as the platform brandmark in the top navigation header and as the architectural layout of the 3D hero system.

### 2.2. Topographic Elevation Contours (Field Map Texture)

- **Geometry:** Smooth, organic mathematical spline curves reminiscent of elevation contours on a geographical field map.
- **Opacity:** $3\%$ to $5\%$ opacity on Ground background (`#FBF8F3`).
- **Application:** Subtle hero backdrop and section dividers, creating depth without visual noise.

### 2.3. Cartographic Grid Lines

- **Geometry:** Fine coordinate crosshairs ($+$) and dotted baseline guides spaced at $48\text{px}$ intervals.
- **Symbolism:** Evokes the precision, dependability, and neutral orientation of an official survey map.
- **Application:** Surrounds the 3D radial area system and service categorization containers.
